GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about RideNest.

Is this a real rental service?

No. RideNest is a student project. It does not reserve real vehicles or collect payments.

Can I compare different vehicle types?

Yes. You can compare cars, bikes, and scooters using the vehicle information page.

Where can I see my booking?

You can check your submitted demo booking on the same device and browser.

What documents are required?

A real rental provider may require valid identification and a driving licence.
